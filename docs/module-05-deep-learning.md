**Weeks 22-26**

Neural networks, computer vision and sequence models for text and time series.

## Week 23

### Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)

## Week 24

### Text Cleaning

Preprocess text before feeding it to models.

### RNN, LSTM, GRU

Sequential models for time series and text.
